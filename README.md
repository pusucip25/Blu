# 🛸 Blu — AI Talking Friend

Your personal AI companion. An animated extraterrestrial character that talks to you — not just answers questions, but acts as your AI agent.

## What is Blu?

Blu is an Android app that brings an AI friend to your pocket. An alien character that:
- 💬 Talks to you naturally
- 🧠 Acts as your personal AI agent
- 🎯 Does things, not just answers questions

## Download

Get the latest APK: [Blu-v1.0.apk](./Blu-v1.0.apk)

## Tech

- **Capacitor** (cross-platform native wrapper)
- **React + TypeScript** (UI)
- **Tailwind CSS** (styling)

## Install

1. Download the APK
2. Enable "Install from unknown sources" on your Android
3. Open the APK to install
